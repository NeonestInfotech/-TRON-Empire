Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TRON Mining Empire a real physical hardware system?

No, TRON Mining Empire operates as a sophisticated Web3 simulation game. Rigs and components represent gamified virtual nodes running within a decentralized environment on the TRON blockchain.

How do I upgrade my virtual hardware rigs?

You can upgrade rigs directly using TRX within the command console interface, overclocking hashrate power, cooling performance, or energy coefficient scales.

Can I run multiple GPU/ASIC installations concurrently?

Yes, once your account Level reaches specific milestones, you unlock the ability to construct multi-GPU clusters to pool hashrate output.

What is the role of the yield progress percentage?

The progression indicator calculates your relative proximity to virtual blocks. Reaching 100% signals block completion, dispersing simulator points to your account balance.

Does this simulate electrical and temperature overhead?

Absolutely. The algorithm integrates hardware thermal dynamics; insufficient cooling reduces active computing power to prevent breakdown simulation events.

Are there transaction fees for deploying machines?

Standard network transaction energy fees on the TRON network apply whenever registering new hardware deployment states on the smart contracts.
